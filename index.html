Built for product-company interviews

Train for real interview rounds

Large product companies, often called FAANG or MAANG, hire through a similar set of rounds. Club practice is planned around each one, so the interview feels familiar when you get there.

Online assessment

Two or three timed coding problems, usually easy to medium. Speed and clean edge-case handling matter most.

DSA interviews

One or more live coding rounds. You explain a brute-force idea, improve it, code it and state the time and space complexity.

System design

Designing a service such as a URL shortener or a chat app: APIs, databases, caching and scaling. Asked more as you gain experience.

Behavioral

Questions about projects, teamwork and mistakes. Answers are built with the STAR method: situation, task, action, result.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who can join?

B.Tech CSE students of NIU in their 1st, 2nd or 3rd year. Intake is limited, and the instructor selects members for each batch.

How do I get an account?

Once you're selected, the instructor gives you the club join code. Open “Member login”, choose “Create account”, and enter your name, roll number, year and the join code.

Which language do we use?

Club sessions are taught in Java. The practice sheets also cover 12 other languages, from C and Python to Go and Rust, plus SQL and system design.

Do I need any prior coding experience?

No. The 1st year batch starts from the basics. Other batches assume you know the topics from earlier steps of the DSA Sheet.

Can the instructor see my progress?

Yes. The instructor sees each member's solved count by step and when they last practiced. Other members only see the leaderboard totals.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

On the login page, type your email and click “Forgot your password?”. A reset link is sent to your inbox.

Already a member?

Log in to see today's progress and pick up where you left off.
